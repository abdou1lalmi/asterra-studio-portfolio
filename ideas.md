# Asterra Studio — Portfolio Design Directions

## Three exploratory directions

| Theme Name | Very Brief Intro | Probability |
|---|---|---:|
| **Architectural Editorial** | An art-directed digital atelier that pairs ink-black type, mineral neutrals, and oversized photography with the clarity of a design journal. It feels cultured, considered, and commercially sharp rather than ornamental. | 0.07 |
| **Soft Technical Modernism** | A precise product-studio language with satin interfaces, graphite grids, and focused blue signals. It would foreground systems thinking and operational confidence. | 0.04 |
| **Warm Neo-Brutalist Salon** | A tactile, poster-like identity with paper grain, vermilion accents, and unapologetic display type. It would be candid, energetic, and highly memorable. | 0.08 |

## Chosen direction — Architectural Editorial

### Design Movement

**Contemporary editorial design with Swiss Modernist proportion and architectural monograph restraint.** The site should read like a crafted studio journal that happens to convert: precise, expressive, and calm.

### Core Principles

1. **Asymmetric narrative composition:** use offset columns, preserved negative space, and intentional cropping rather than a sequence of identical centered cards.
2. **Material contrast:** layer warm paper, soft silver, saturated ink, and a single vivid signal color so the interface feels physical without visual noise.
3. **Case-study first:** show the work as evidence, with services and credentials providing supporting structure rather than competing with it.
4. **Measured motion:** animate entrances, image masks, and hover states to clarify hierarchy; avoid decorative loops, scroll-jacking, or visual spectacle without purpose.

### Color Philosophy

The visual world moves between **bone paper** (approachable intelligence), **obsidian ink** (authority), **silvered graphite** (technical craft), and **signal chartreuse** (decisive action). The accent should appear sparingly as a high-value editorial annotation, never as a generic gradient.

### Layout Paradigm

The page is built as a **vertical editorial sequence**: a narrow, utility-led header opens into an asymmetric hero; a large image-led work strip interrupts the rhythm; expertise is presented as a numbered margin note; then a split credibility/contact close. No centered “template stack” or repeated white-card grid dominates the experience.

### Signature Elements

1. A narrow **vertical index rail** and microscopic page coordinates that frame major sections.
2. **Image crops inside fine graphite keylines**, with image counters and category labels treated like museum captions.
3. A small chartreuse **asterisk/star mark** used as the brand symbol, navigation locator, and CTA punctuation.

### Interaction Philosophy

Interactions should feel like handling a printed portfolio: calm lift on work tiles, underline reveal on text links, a measured menu state, and a decisive solid button press. Every interaction has a clear destination, keyboard focus remains visible, and nonessential animation yields to `prefers-reduced-motion`.

### Animation

Sections reveal via 20–36px vertical translation and opacity over 500–700ms with a stagger of 50–75ms. Project imagery uses masked-scale hover treatment while captions slide 8px upward. Navigation and buttons use 150–220ms transitions with the custom ease-out curve. Motion is disabled or simplified for reduced-motion preferences.

### Typography System

**DM Serif Display** handles the editorial headline and case-study names in a controlled, high-contrast serif. **Manrope** handles all navigation, metadata, paragraphs, and controls for contemporary precision. Headlines use deliberate line breaks and a 0.9–1.0 line height; UI labels use uppercase, tracking, and compact sizing only where it adds information hierarchy.

### Brand Essence

**Asterra is a product and brand studio for ambitious teams that need their digital presence to feel as intelligent as the work behind it.**

**Personality:** exacting, luminous, quietly bold.

### Brand Voice

Headlines are declarative and specific. CTAs are invitations to make an important decision, never generic filler. Microcopy is concise, observant, and human.

> “Products worth choosing need a world worth entering.”

> “Bring the hard brief. We’ll make it legible.”

### Wordmark & Logo

The wordmark uses a restrained “Asterra” lockup with a geometric **four-point asterisk / compass mark** that suggests direction and a fixed point. The mark appears without text in the navigation and favicon treatment.

### Signature Brand Color

**Aster Signal — #D7FF38.**

## Style Decisions

- Signal chartreuse operates as an editorial annotation first: the compass mark, one decisive action, selected project numerals, and the single end-of-page conversion field.
- Every major section carries a visible coordinate device—vertical rail, counter, caption, or margin note—to reinforce the studio-monograph structure.
- Case studies remain the evidence layer. Practice and service material should read as precise supporting marginalia rather than a generic agency capabilities grid.
